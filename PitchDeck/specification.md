# Pitch Deck Presentation Design Specification

## Overview
Persuasive and concise presentation design for startup pitches, investor presentations, and funding rounds.

## Design Principles
- **Concise**: Communicate maximum value in minimum time
- **Compelling**: Tell a story that resonates with investors
- **Professional**: Balance creativity with credibility
- **Data-Driven**: Back claims with solid metrics

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: Bold, confident colors reflecting brand
- **Professional**: Deep blues, blacks for credibility
- **Accent**: Bright colors for key metrics and CTAs
- **Background**: Clean whites or subtle gradients
- **Highlight**: Use for traction, growth, and success metrics

## Typography
- **Headings**: Strong, confident fonts
  - Company Name: 48-56pt, Bold
  - Slide Titles: 32-40pt, Semi-bold
  - Tagline: 24-28pt, Regular
- **Body Text**: 18-22pt, Regular (minimal text)
- **Numbers**: 56-72pt, Bold for key metrics
- **Footnotes**: 10-12pt for disclaimers

## Layout Guidelines
- **One Message Per Slide**: Single clear point
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Numbers and key points prominent
- **Consistent**: Same layout for similar content types
- **Breathing Room**: Don't overcrowd slides

## Standard Pitch Deck Structure
1. **Company/Vision Slide**: Logo, tagline, big vision
2. **Problem Slide**: Pain point you're solving
3. **Solution Slide**: Your product/service
4. **Market Opportunity**: TAM/SAM/SOM
5. **Product/Demo**: How it works
6. **Business Model**: How you make money
7. **Traction**: Growth metrics and milestones
8. **Competition**: Competitive landscape and differentiation
9. **Go-to-Market Strategy**: Customer acquisition plan
10. **Team Slide**: Founders and key team members
11. **Financial Projections**: Revenue forecasts (3-5 years)
12. **The Ask**: Funding amount and use of funds
13. **Contact/Thank You**: Contact information

## Visual Elements
- **Product Screenshots**: High-quality UI/UX images
- **Charts**: Growth trends, market size, projections
- **Logos**: Partners, investors, customers (social proof)
- **Team Photos**: Professional headshots
- **Mockups**: Product in use, customer scenarios
- **Comparison Tables**: Competitive positioning
- **Timelines**: Roadmap and milestones

## Key Metrics to Highlight
- **Traction Numbers**: Users, revenue, growth rate
- **Market Size**: TAM, SAM, SOM with sources
- **Unit Economics**: CAC, LTV, margins
- **Growth Metrics**: MoM or YoY growth
- **Engagement**: Retention, active users
- **Partnerships**: Strategic relationships

## Content Guidelines
- Keep text minimal (no paragraphs)
- Use bullet points sparingly (3-5 max)
- Lead with numbers and metrics
- Tell a cohesive story
- Address investor concerns proactively
- Be realistic with projections
- Show, don't just tell

## Best Practices
- 10-15 slides maximum (10-20 minute pitch)
- Start with a hook or powerful statistic
- Use customer testimonials or case studies
- Show traction and momentum
- Demonstrate market understanding
- Highlight competitive advantages
- Include clear call-to-action
- Prepare for questions
- Have backup slides for deep dives

## Team Slide Guidelines
- Include relevant experience
- Show domain expertise
- Highlight previous successes
- Demonstrate complementary skills
- Add advisory board if notable

## Financial Slide Tips
- Conservative projections
- Show unit economics
- Include key assumptions
- Compare to industry benchmarks
- Explain revenue streams

## Investor-Specific Considerations
- **Seed Stage**: Focus on problem, solution, and team
- **Series A**: Emphasize traction and growth
- **Series B+**: Show scalability and market capture
- **Angels**: More personal story and vision
- **VCs**: Data-driven, scalability, returns

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Too much text on slides
- Overly optimistic projections
- Ignoring competition
- Unclear business model
- Weak problem statement
- No clear ask
- Poor design quality

## File Requirements
- **Format**: .pptx and .pdf versions
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
- **File Size**: Optimized for email (<10MB)
- **Versions**: Pitch version and detailed appendix
- **Naming Convention**: `CompanyName_PitchDeck_Date_Round.pptx`

## Supporting Materials
- Executive summary (1-2 pages)
- Financial model spreadsheet
- Product demo video
- Market research data
- Customer testimonials
- Press coverage
