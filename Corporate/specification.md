# Corporate Presentation Design Specification

## Overview
Professional presentation design for corporate communications, board meetings, quarterly reviews, and internal/external business presentations.

## Design Principles
- **Professional**: Maintain a polished, business-appropriate aesthetic
- **Brand-Aligned**: Strictly follow company brand guidelines
- **Data-Driven**: Emphasize clarity in data visualization
- **Minimal**: Avoid clutter and unnecessary decorative elements

## Color Palette
- **Primary Colors**: Corporate brand colors (typically blues, grays)
- **Secondary Colors**: Complementary professional tones
- **Accent Colors**: Use sparingly for emphasis
- **Background**: White or light gray (#F5F5F5)

## Typography
- **Headings**: Sans-serif fonts (Arial, Calibri, Helvetica)
  - Title: 44-48pt, Bold
  - Section Headers: 32-36pt, Semi-bold
  - Subsections: 24-28pt, Regular
- **Body Text**: 18-24pt, Regular
- **Footnotes/Citations**: 12-14pt, Light

## Layout Guidelines
- **Margins**: Minimum 0.5" on all sides
- **Grid System**: Use 12-column grid for alignment
- **White Space**: At least 30% of slide should be white space
- **Logo Placement**: Top-left or bottom-right corner

## Slide Structure
1. **Title Slide**: Company logo, presentation title, presenter name, date
2. **Agenda Slide**: Clear outline of topics
3. **Content Slides**: One main idea per slide
4. **Section Dividers**: Visual breaks between major topics
5. **Closing Slide**: Contact information and call-to-action

## Visual Elements
- **Images**: High-resolution, professional stock photos or company imagery
- **Icons**: Flat, line-style icons matching brand guidelines
- **Charts/Graphs**: Clean, labeled clearly, use corporate colors
- **Tables**: Minimal borders, alternating row colors for readability

## Best Practices
- Limit bullet points to 5-7 per slide
- Use data visualization instead of raw numbers
- Include slide numbers for reference
- Maintain consistent header/footer across all slides
- Use animations sparingly and professionally

## File Requirements
- **Format**: .pptx or .pdf
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
- **File Size**: Optimize images to keep under 50MB
- **Naming Convention**: `YYYY-MM-DD_Topic_Presenter.pptx`
