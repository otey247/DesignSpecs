# Strategic Innovation: PowerPoint Theme Specification

This theme is designed for high-stakes corporate strategy and technology partnership presentations. It balances a professional, authoritative deep navy foundation with a modern, agile teal accent to communicate transformation, intelligent automation, and human-centric design.

---

## 1. Global Theme Colors

The palette relies on a "High-Contrast Corporate" scheme, utilizing deep values for authority and bright cyan-teals for "innovation" highlights.

| Slot | Color Name | Hex | RGB | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Background 1** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | (255, 255, 255) | Primary slide background. |
| **Text 1** | Deep Navy | `#002D5B` | (0, 45, 91) | Primary titles and body text. |
| **Accent 1** | Innovation Teal | `#00A3DA` | (0, 163, 218) | Hero metrics, icons, and action-oriented text. |
| **Accent 2** | Soft Sky Blue | `#D1EBF7` | (209, 235, 247) | Logo watermark and secondary shape fills. |
| **Accent 3** | Slate Gray | `#4D4D4D` | (77, 77, 77) | Secondary body text and descriptors. |
| **Accent 4** | Light Silver | `#F2F2F2` | (242, 242, 242) | Box backgrounds and neutral UI elements. |
| **Accent 5** | Medium Gray | `#A6A6A6` | (166, 166, 166) | Arrow connectors and inactive process steps. |

---

## 2. Typography Hierarchy

The theme utilizes a geometric sans-serif typeface (Closest Google Font: **Montserrat** or **Inter**) to maintain a clean, architectural feel.

*   **Slide Titles**
    *   **Font:** Montserrat Bold
    *   **Size:** 36pt – 40pt
    *   **Color:** Deep Navy (`#002D5B`)
    *   **Alignment:** Left-aligned, positioned above a horizontal divider.
*   **Subheaders / Section Titles**
    *   **Font:** Montserrat Bold
    *   **Size:** 20pt – 24pt
    *   **Color:** Deep Navy or Innovation Teal
*   **Body Text**
    *   **Font:** Montserrat Medium / Regular
    *   **Size:** 14pt – 18pt
    *   **Color:** Slate Gray (`#4D4D4D`) or Deep Navy
*   **Hero Metrics / Data Highlights**
    *   **Font:** Montserrat Extra Bold
    *   **Size:** 60pt+
    *   **Color:** Innovation Teal (`#00A3DA`)
    *   **Effect:** Often used for "Before/After" transitions (e.g., "Days $\rightarrow$ Minutes").

---

## 3. Master Slide Layouts

### Title Slide
*   **Composition:** Vertical split.
*   **Left Pane (40%):** Deep Navy fill with white text for the main title.
*   **Right Pane (60%):** White background with Teal sub-text. 
*   **Branding:** A large, faded "Spade" logo watermark in Soft Sky Blue (`#D1EBF7`) in the bottom right corner.

### Standard Content Slide
*   **Margins:** Generous top and side margins (approx. 0.75").
*   **Header:** Title text sits top-left, underlined by a 1pt horizontal line in Innovation Teal that spans the width of the slide.
*   **Watermark:** The "Spade" icon is consistently placed in the bottom right corner at approx. 15% opacity.

### Comparison / Pivot Slide
*   **Logic:** A two-column layout separated by a central "Strategic Pivot" arrow in Innovation Teal. 
*   **Visual Weight:** Uses a dark gray "Legacy" box on the left vs. a vibrant Teal-bordered "Future" box on the right.

---

## 4. Graphic & Shape Styles

*   **Iconography:** Outline-style icons with a consistent stroke weight (approx. 2pt). All icons utilize the Innovation Teal color.
*   **Shape Language:** 
    *   **Hexagons:** Used for "Team" or "Ecosystem" representations to imply connectivity and strength.
    *   **Chevrons:** Used for process flows, indicating forward momentum.
    *   **Rounded Rectangles:** Used for roadmap steps with a corner radius of approx. 0.1".
*   **Connectors:** Solid teal arrows for direct transitions; dashed teal lines for "journeys" or "roadmaps."
*   **Gradients:** Subtle linear gradients are used only in "The Magic Middle" or transformation icons to indicate AI/Automation processing.

---

## 5. Compositional Rules

*   **Visual Flow:** Information generally flows from Left to Right (Chronological) or Top to Bottom (Hierarchy).
*   **Whitespace:** High emphasis on "Active Whitespace." Slides are never cluttered; each slide focuses on a single core concept or metric.
*   **Alignment:** Strict left-justification for all text blocks to maintain a clean vertical axis.
*   **The "Rule of Three":** Frequently uses three-column layouts for "The Goal / The Win / The Pivot" or "Stage 1 / Stage 2 / Stage 3" to ensure digestibility.
*   **Emphasis:** Use *Italics* within deep navy text blocks for quotes or "Human Voice" insights to differentiate from technical data.
