# Marketing Presentation Design Specification

## Overview
Compelling and persuasive presentation design for product launches, campaigns, sales presentations, and marketing initiatives.

## Design Principles
- **Bold**: Eye-catching and memorable design
- **Persuasive**: Drive action and emotional connection
- **On-Brand**: Strong brand identity throughout
- **Visual-First**: Prioritize imagery over text

## Color Palette
- **Brand Colors**: Dominant use of brand identity colors
- **Emotional Colors**: Colors that evoke desired feelings
  - Trust: Blues
  - Energy: Reds, Oranges
  - Growth: Greens
  - Luxury: Purples, Golds
- **Contrast**: High contrast for impact
- **Gradients**: Modern gradients for visual interest

## Typography
- **Headings**: Bold, impactful fonts
  - Hero Text: 56-72pt, Extra Bold
  - Headlines: 36-44pt, Bold
  - Subheadings: 24-32pt, Semi-bold
- **Body Text**: 18-22pt, Regular (minimal body text)
- **Callouts**: Large, attention-grabbing text

## Layout Guidelines
- **Asymmetry**: Dynamic, non-traditional layouts
- **Full-Bleed Images**: Edge-to-edge imagery for impact
- **Z-Pattern**: Guide eye flow naturally
- **Negative Space**: Strategic use for emphasis

## Slide Structure
1. **Hero Slide**: Powerful opening visual with key message
2. **Problem Slide**: Identify customer pain points
3. **Solution Slides**: Showcase product/service benefits
4. **Social Proof**: Testimonials, case studies, metrics
5. **Features/Benefits**: Visual comparison
6. **Call-to-Action**: Clear next steps
7. **Contact Slide**: Multiple ways to engage

## Visual Elements
- **Product Photography**: High-quality, lifestyle-oriented
- **Video Content**: Short, engaging product demos
- **Customer Photos**: Real people using products
- **Statistics**: Large, bold numbers with context
- **Mockups**: Products in real-world settings
- **Animations**: Dynamic transitions for energy
- **Icons**: Modern, branded icon sets

## Content Strategy
- Focus on benefits, not just features
- Use storytelling techniques
- Include social proof and testimonials
- Emphasize ROI and value proposition
- Create urgency when appropriate
- Address objections proactively

## Best Practices
- Limit text to key messages
- Use powerful imagery on every slide
- Include brand elements consistently
- Create visual hierarchy with size and color
- Use animations to reveal information progressively
- Test emotional impact on target audience
- Include clear calls-to-action throughout

## Metrics to Highlight
- ROI and cost savings
- Performance improvements
- Customer satisfaction scores
- Market share or growth
- Awards and recognition
- Client/customer counts

## File Requirements
- **Format**: .pptx with high-res images
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 or 4K for large screens
- **File Organization**: Keep source files and assets
- **Brand Assets**: Include logo variations and guidelines
- **Naming Convention**: `Campaign_Product_Date_Version.pptx`
